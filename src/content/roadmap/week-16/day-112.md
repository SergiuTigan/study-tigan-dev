## What You're Doing

Ship your final project. But today is about more than just Project #3 — it's about completing your entire portfolio. By tonight, you'll have three deployed, documented, demo'd AI projects. That's the foundation of your job search.

## The Work

### Hours 1-2: Best README Yet (120 min)

Project #3's README should be portfolio-showcase quality:

**Everything from Projects 1 and 2 plus:**
- **Architecture section with diagram** — Mermaid, Excalidraw, or professional image
- **AI deep dive** — detailed explanation of the advanced pattern you used
- **Performance section** — response times, accuracy metrics, cost per interaction
- **Design decisions** — why you chose this architecture with tradeoffs discussed
- **Demo section** — embedded GIF or video thumbnail with link

**The "Featured Project" Test:**
Would a recruiter reading only this README want to schedule an interview? If yes, ship it. If no, iterate.

### Hours 3-4: Best Demo Video Yet (120 min)

This video will likely be the first thing hiring managers see. Make it exceptional.

**Structure:**
- **0:00-0:10** — One-sentence hook: what this is and why it matters
- **0:10-0:45** — Problem setup: what challenge does this solve?
- **0:45-1:45** — Live demo: walk through the core flow, show the AI in action
- **1:45-2:15** — Technical depth: architecture, AI pattern, why this approach
- **2:15-2:45** — Results: metrics, what you learned, what you'd improve
- **2:45-3:00** — Close: your background, what you're looking for, where to find you

**Video Production Tips:**
- Script it word for word
- Record 3+ takes
- Loom Pro has basic editing (or use iMovie/CapCut)
- Clean desktop, no notifications
- Good audio matters more than good video
- Smile at the camera in the intro and close
