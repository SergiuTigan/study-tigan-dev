## The Final Project

This is it. Your third and most ambitious project. After this week, you'll have a complete portfolio — three shipped AI-powered applications, each demonstrating different skills, patterns, and problem domains.

Project #3 should be your masterpiece. Not necessarily the most complex — but the most polished, the most thoughtful, and the one that best represents who you are as an AI engineer.

## Choosing Project #3

By now you've used two from your shortlist (Dobby AI, Iron Pulse AI Coach, AI Customer Support Platform, Luppy AI Vet Assistant). Pick your third based on:

1. **What's missing from your portfolio?** If you've built two consumer apps, make this one B2B. If you've done two chatbots, make this a tool/dashboard.
2. **What's the strongest demo?** This will likely be the project you show first in interviews.
3. **What technical depth can you add?** Multi-model orchestration, advanced RAG, evaluation pipeline, real-time features — push yourself.

## Portfolio Composition Check

After this week, your three projects should cover:

- [ ] At least two different AI patterns (chat completion, RAG, tool use, agents)
- [ ] At least two different problem domains
- [ ] At least one project with measurable metrics
- [ ] At least one project a non-technical person could understand
- [ ] At least one project that shows technical sophistication
