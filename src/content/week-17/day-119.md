## What You're Doing

Two big tasks today. First: create a one-page PDF resume tailored for AI engineer roles. Second: set up the job alert infrastructure that will feed your applications next week. This is the foundation of your active job search.

## The Work

### Hours 1-3: Resume (180 min)

**Format:** One page. PDF. Clean design. No gimmicks.

**Template tool:** Use Jake's Resume (LaTeX), Novoresume, or a clean Google Docs template. Avoid fancy designs — ATS systems can't parse them.

**Structure:**

```
[YOUR NAME]
AI Engineer | Full-Stack TypeScript + LLMs
[city] · [email] · [phone] · [linkedin] · [github] · [portfolio]

SUMMARY (2-3 lines)
AI Engineer with [X] years of full-stack development experience.
Shipped 3 AI-powered applications using LLMs, RAG, and modern
web technologies. Seeking AI engineer roles building intelligent
products.

PROJECTS
[Project 1 Name] — [Live URL]
• [What it does in one line]
• [Key technical detail: "Built RAG pipeline with 95% retrieval accuracy"]
• [Stack: Next.js, OpenAI, Supabase, Vercel]

[Project 2 Name] — [Live URL]
• [What it does in one line]
• [Key technical detail]
• [Stack]

[Project 3 Name] — [Live URL]
• [What it does in one line]
• [Key technical detail]
• [Stack]

EXPERIENCE
[Most Recent Role] — [Company] — [Dates]
• [Achievement with numbers, reframed for AI relevance]
• [Achievement with numbers]
• [Achievement with numbers]

[Previous Role] — [Company] — [Dates]
• [Achievement with numbers]
• [Achievement with numbers]

SKILLS
AI/ML: LLMs, RAG, Prompt Engineering, AI Agents, Embeddings
Languages: TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, SQL
Frameworks: Next.js, React, Node.js, Angular
AI Tools: OpenAI API, Anthropic API, Vercel AI SDK, LangChain
Databases: PostgreSQL, Supabase, Pinecone, Prisma
DevOps: Vercel, Docker, Git, CI/CD

EDUCATION
[Degree] — [University] — [Year]
```

**Resume Quality Checks:**
- [ ] One page exactly (no more)
- [ ] Projects section is prominent (not buried after experience)
- [ ] Every bullet starts with a strong verb (Built, Designed, Shipped, Optimized)
- [ ] Numbers in at least 50% of bullets
- [ ] Links are included and work
- [ ] ATS-friendly format (no images, columns, or fancy formatting)
- [ ] Saved as PDF
- [ ] File name: `FirstName-LastName-AI-Engineer-Resume.pdf`

### Hours 4-5: Job Alert Infrastructure (120 min)

Set up alerts on every relevant platform so opportunities come to you:

**Wellfound (AngelList)**
- Create/update profile with AI engineer focus
- Set job preferences: AI Engineer, Full-Stack Engineer, Software Engineer
- Filter: Remote, [your city], startup stage preferences
- Turn on email alerts

**Y Combinator Work at a Startup (WaaS)**
- Create profile
- Filter for AI/ML companies
- Set alerts for new postings

**LinkedIn Jobs**
- Search: "AI Engineer", "LLM Engineer", "Full-Stack AI"
- Set up 3-4 saved searches with email alerts
- Filter: Remote + your location, experience level, date posted

**Direct Company Careers Pages — Bookmark These:**

*Tier 1 (Dream companies):*
- Anthropic careers
- OpenAI careers
- Vercel careers
- Cohere careers

*Tier 2 (YC and growth-stage startups):*
- Browse YC company directory filtered by AI
- Check who's hiring on HN "Who's Hiring" threads
- Series A-C AI startups

*Tier 3 (Established tech):*
- Google AI roles
- Microsoft AI roles
- Established tech companies with AI teams

**Other Platforms:**
- levels.fyi — for salary data and job listings
- Hacker News monthly "Who's Hiring" thread
- X/Twitter — follow AI company accounts, #hiring hashtags
- Discord/Slack communities — AI engineer channels

**Networking Setup:**
- Follow 20-30 AI engineers on LinkedIn
- Follow 10-15 AI companies
- Join 2-3 relevant communities (Discord, Slack, or Reddit)
- Turn on notifications for target companies' LinkedIn posts

**Create a Tracking Spreadsheet:**
```
| Company | Role | Platform | Date Applied | Status | Contact | Notes |
```

You'll use this every day next week.

## Key Insight

The resume is your foot in the door, but the portfolio is what keeps the door open. Notice the resume structure puts PROJECTS before EXPERIENCE. That's intentional. Your three AI projects are more relevant than any previous role for the positions you're targeting.
