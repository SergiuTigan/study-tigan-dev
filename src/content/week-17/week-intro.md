## Why Branding Matters

You have three shipped AI projects. You have real skills. But none of that matters if no one knows about it.

This week you transform your online presence from "Senior Angular Developer" to "AI Engineer." Every platform, every profile, every piece of content should tell the same story: you're a full-stack engineer who builds AI-powered products, and you're ready to do it professionally.

## The Story You're Telling

Every piece of content this week should reinforce one narrative:

**"I'm a senior engineer with [X] years of experience who spent 4 months systematically building AI engineering skills. I shipped 3 production-quality projects. I understand LLMs, RAG, agents, and AI system design. I'm looking for an AI engineer role where I can build AI-powered products."**

That's the story. LinkedIn tells it. GitHub proves it. Blog posts deepen it. Resume packages it.

## What You're NOT Doing

- You're not pretending to be something you're not
- You're not hiding your Angular/frontend background (it's a strength)
- You're not being humble about what you built (false modesty loses interviews)
- You're not mass-applying yet (that's next week)

This week is pure positioning. Make yourself findable, credible, and interesting.
