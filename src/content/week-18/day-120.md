**Topic:** Research + Targeted Applications
**Phase:** Faza 4 — Portfolio + Job Hunt · Week 18

---

## What You're Doing

Today you establish the daily application routine you'll follow Monday through Thursday. Each day: research companies, identify roles, customize and submit 5-7 quality applications. No spray-and-pray. Every application is intentional.

## The Routine (Use This Every Day This Week)

### Block 1: Research (30 min)

**Find 5-7 companies to apply to today.**

Sources to check daily (rotate through these):
- LinkedIn saved searches (check new postings)
- Wellfound/AngelList new listings
- YC WaaS new companies
- HN "Who's Hiring" thread
- Target company career pages
- X/Twitter #hiring posts

**For each company, spend 3-5 minutes:**
- What does the company do?
- What's their AI product/feature?
- What does the role actually need? (Read beyond the title)
- Who's the hiring manager or team lead? (LinkedIn search)
- Is there a connection you can leverage?

**Quick Qualify/Disqualify:**
- Does the role match your skills (70%+ overlap)? → Apply
- Does it require 5+ years of ML/PhD? → Skip
- Is it clearly a data science or research role? → Skip
- Is it an AI engineering or full-stack AI role? → Apply

### Block 2: Customize + Apply (90 min)

**For each application (15-20 min each):**

**Step 1: Customize the cover letter/intro (5 min)**

Template:
```
Hi [Team/Hiring Manager],

I'm an AI Engineer with [X] years of full-stack experience, transitioning
from senior frontend development to building AI-powered products full-time.

I'm excited about [Company] because [specific reason — product, mission,
or technology]. My project [Project Name] is directly relevant — it uses
[relevant tech] to [solve similar problem]. You can see it live at [URL].

My portfolio includes 3 deployed AI applications covering chat completion,
RAG pipelines, and [third pattern]. I've documented the technical journey
in my blog: [Blog URL].

I'd love to discuss how my full-stack + AI skills could contribute to
[Company]'s [specific team/product].

[Your name]
[LinkedIn] · [GitHub] · [Portfolio]
```

**Step 2: Adjust resume emphasis (3 min)**
- If the role emphasizes RAG → lead with your RAG project
- If the role emphasizes agents → lead with your agent project
- If the role emphasizes full-stack → emphasize the complete app architecture

**Step 3: Submit (5 min)**
- Attach PDF resume
- Paste customized cover letter
- Include portfolio links
- Double-check everything before hitting submit

**Step 4: Log it (2 min)**
- Add to tracking spreadsheet
- Note the platform, date, role, and any contact name
- Set a reminder for follow-up in 5-7 days
