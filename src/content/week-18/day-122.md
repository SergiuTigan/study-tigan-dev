## What You're Doing

Day 3 of applications. Today, add a new layer: warm outreach. For every company you apply to, check if you know someone there or can find a connection. A referral dramatically increases your chances.

## The Routine

Same structure:
- **30 min:** Research + connection mapping

## Today's Added Element: Warm Outreach

**For each target company, check:**
1. Do you know someone who works there? (LinkedIn connections)
2. Does a 2nd-degree connection work there? (Ask for an intro)
3. Is the hiring manager active on LinkedIn? (Comment on their posts first, then reach out)
4. Did anyone from your communities mention this company?

**Warm Outreach Template:**
```
Hi [Name],

I noticed you work at [Company] — I'm really impressed by [specific
product/feature]. I'm an AI Engineer (transitioning from senior
frontend) and I've applied for the [Role] position.

I shipped 3 AI-powered applications recently, including [Project]
which uses [relevant technology]. Would you be open to a quick chat
about what it's like working on [Company]'s AI team?

Here's my portfolio: [GitHub/LinkedIn]

Thanks either way!
[Your name]
```

**Rules for outreach:**
- Be specific about why you're reaching out
- Reference their work or the company's product
- Include a link to your work (don't make them search)
- Don't ask for a referral directly on first message
- Follow up once after 5 days if no response

## Key Insight

Warm outreach isn't networking for the sake of networking. It's strategically increasing your odds. A referred candidate is 4-5x more likely to get an interview than a cold applicant. Even if only 1 out of 3 outreach messages leads somewhere, that's still better odds than any job board.
