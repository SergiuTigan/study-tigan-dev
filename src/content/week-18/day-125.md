# Day 125 — Batch Applications + Follow-ups

## What You're Doing

Two goals today. First: batch-process remaining applications to hit your 30+ target. Second: follow up on Monday-Tuesday's applications. The follow-up is often what converts a silent application into a conversation.

## The Work

### Hour 1: Batch Applications (60 min)

You have your system. Use it at speed:

- Work through your remaining target companies list
- Use your best cover letter template (you know which version works now)
- Apply to 5-8 companies in focused sequence
- Quick customization per application (company name, relevant project, specific reason)

**Batch application efficiency tips:**
- Have your resume PDF open and ready
- Keep cover letter template in a doc, edit company-specific lines only
- Have project URLs bookmarked for quick paste
- Use keyboard shortcuts for form filling

### Hour 2: Follow-ups (60 min)

**For Monday/Tuesday applications (5-7 day old):**

Check your spreadsheet. For each application that hasn't received a response:

**Follow-up email template (if you have a contact):**
```
Subject: Following up — [Role] application

Hi [Name],

I applied for the [Role] position at [Company] on [date] and wanted
to follow up. I'm particularly excited about [specific thing about
the company/role].

Since applying, I've also [published a technical blog post about
my RAG implementation / added a new feature to my portfolio project /
relevant update].

Happy to hop on a quick call at your convenience.

[Your name]
[LinkedIn] · [GitHub]
```

**LinkedIn follow-up (if no email contact):**
- Find the recruiter or hiring manager on LinkedIn
- Connect with a note referencing your application
- Keep it brief and professional

**Follow-up rules:**
- One follow-up after 5-7 days is professional, not pushy
- Don't follow up more than twice total
- Always add value in the follow-up (new info, relevant article, project update)
- If they said "we'll be in touch," wait their timeline before following up

### Hour 3: More Applications (60 min)

Continue applying. Focus on any platforms you haven't fully explored:
- Check company career pages directly (not just job boards)
- Look at recent X/Twitter posts from AI companies saying "we're hiring"
- Check Slack/Discord communities for job postings
- Apply to any roles from your warm outreach conversations

## Key Insight

Follow-ups work. Studies show that 80% of deals require 5+ follow-ups, but 44% of people give up after one attempt. A polite, value-adding follow-up puts you ahead of almost every other applicant. Most people apply and forget. Don't be most people.
