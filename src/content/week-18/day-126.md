# Day 126 — Batch Applications + Networking

> *"50 quality applications with a strong portfolio beats 500 generic ones with nothing to show."*

**Date:** Sunday, 21 September 2025
**Hours:** 5h · Full session
**Topic:** Final Application Push & Network Building
**Phase:** Faza 4 — Portfolio + Job Hunt · Week 18

---

## What You're Doing

Final application day of the week. Two objectives: hit your 30-50 application target and invest seriously in networking. Applications get you into the pipeline. Networking gets you to the top of it.

## The Work

### Hours 1-2: Application Sprint (120 min)

Push to close the gap between where you are and 30-50 total.

**If you're at 25-30:** Excellent. Aim for 40.
**If you're at 30-40:** Great. Aim for 50.
**If you're at 20-25:** You need to move fast. Focus on speed without sacrificing basic customization.

**Speed mode application (10 min each):**
1. Scan the role (2 min) — is it a match?
2. Customize cover letter top paragraph (3 min) — company name + why
3. Attach resume + project links (2 min)
4. Submit + log (3 min)

### Hours 3-4: LinkedIn Networking (120 min)

Networking isn't about asking for favors. It's about building relationships and visibility.

**Active Engagement (45 min):**
- Comment thoughtfully on 10-15 posts from AI engineers, AI company leaders, and recruiters
- Not "Great post!" — actual insights, questions, or additions
- Your comments show up in other people's feeds. This is free visibility.

**Connection Requests (30 min):**
Send connection requests to:
- AI engineers at target companies
- AI engineering content creators
- Recruiters who post AI roles
- People who engaged with your blog posts

**Always include a note:**
```
Hi [Name], I've been following your work on [topic]. I recently
transitioned to AI engineering and shipped 3 AI-powered apps.
Would love to connect and learn from your experience in the space.
```

**Content Sharing (30 min):**
Post on LinkedIn:
```
Week 18 of my AI engineering transition:

- 3 AI projects shipped (all live with demos)
- [X] applications sent this week
- Published technical deep dive on [topic]

The biggest lesson? [One insight from your journey]

If you're hiring AI engineers or know someone who is,
I'd love to connect: [LinkedIn URL]

#AIEngineering #CareerTransition #OpenToWork
```

This kind of post gets engagement. People root for career changers.

**Community Engagement (15 min):**
- Post an introduction in 1-2 AI engineering communities
- Share your blog post in relevant channels
- Answer questions where you can (this builds reputation)

### Hour 5: Week Wrap-Up (60 min)

**Application Audit:**
- [ ] Total applications counted and logged
- [ ] All entries in spreadsheet are current
- [ ] Follow-up dates set for this week's applications
- [ ] Any responses flagged and prioritized

**Platform Check:**
- [ ] LinkedIn profile still current (check view count — is it increasing?)
- [ ] Wellfound/AngelList profile getting views?
- [ ] Any inbound messages or recruiter outreach?

**Response Plan:**
For any responses you've received:
- Reply within 24 hours
- Express enthusiasm
- Be available for next steps
- Prepare to discuss your projects in detail

**Next Week Prep:**
Next week is interview prep. But if interviews start coming in sooner, be ready:
- Can you explain each project in 2 minutes?
- Can you whiteboard a system design?
- Can you answer "Tell me about yourself" in 90 seconds?

## Key Insight

The application phase is a numbers game with a quality floor. Below the floor (generic, untargeted applications), more volume doesn't help. Above the floor (customized, portfolio-backed applications), more volume directly correlates with more interviews. You've been above the floor all week. Now the math is on your side.
