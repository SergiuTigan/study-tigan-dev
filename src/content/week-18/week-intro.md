# Week 18 — Apply: 30-50 Quality Applications

> *"Applying to jobs isn't a lottery. It's a sales process. Quality over quantity — but you still need volume."*

**Dates:** 15-21 September 2025
**Hours:** 15h total
**Phase:** Faza 4 — Portfolio + Job Hunt
**Focus:** Targeted Job Applications

---

## The Goal

30-50 quality applications by Sunday night. Not spray-and-pray. Each application should be at least partially customized. Every cover letter should mention the company by name. Every resume should highlight the most relevant project.

## The Strategy

### Company Tiers

**Tier 1 — Dream Companies (5-10 applications)**
Anthropic, OpenAI, Vercel, Cohere, Replicate, AI-focused labs.
These are reach applications. Apply anyway. Your portfolio makes you a credible candidate.

**Tier 2 — AI-First Startups (15-25 applications)**
YC companies, Series A-C startups building AI products, AI tooling companies.
This is your sweet spot. Companies that need engineers who can build full AI products.

**Tier 3 — Established Tech with AI Teams (10-15 applications)**
Google, Microsoft, Stripe, Datadog — established companies with AI initiatives.
These have structured hiring but value full-stack + AI combination.

### Application Quality Checklist

Every single application should include:
- [ ] Customized headline that matches the role title
- [ ] 2-3 sentences about why THIS company
- [ ] Reference to the most relevant project
- [ ] Link to live demo, GitHub, and blog post
- [ ] Resume PDF attached

## Tracking

Update your spreadsheet daily:
```
| # | Company | Role | Tier | Platform | Applied | Status | Follow-up | Notes |
```

Track everything. You'll need this data for follow-ups and to spot patterns.

## Mindset

Rejection is the default. Not because you're not good enough — because hiring is noisy. Your goal is to get enough quality applications out that the math works in your favor. If 5% of applications lead to interviews, you need 40+ applications to get 2+ interviews.
