## What You're Doing

Today you prepare for the behavioral side of interviews. Every company — from startups to FAANG — will ask behavioral questions. "Tell me about a time when..." questions. "Why are you making this transition?" questions. "Tell me about yourself" questions.

Your AI projects are your STAR stories. Your transition is your narrative. Prepare them today so they flow naturally in the moment.

## The Work

### 1. The 90-Second Pitch (30 min)

This is your answer to "Tell me about yourself." Memorize the structure, not the words:

**Structure:**
```
[Present] I'm an AI Engineer focused on building AI-powered applications
with LLMs, RAG, and modern web technologies.

[Past] I spent [X] years as a Senior [Frontend/Full-Stack] Engineer
at [Company], where I built [brief impressive thing].

[Transition] Earlier this year, I spent 4 months doing a structured
deep dive into AI engineering — not just tutorials, but shipping
real products.

[Proof] I built and deployed 3 AI applications: [Project 1] which
uses [pattern], [Project 2] which uses [pattern], and [Project 3]
which [pattern]. All live, documented, with demo videos.

[Future] I'm looking for a role where I can build AI products
full-time. I'm particularly excited about [Company/role] because
[specific reason].
```

**Practice this out loud 5 times.** It should be 60-90 seconds. Not scripted-sounding, but structured. Hit every point without rambling.

### 2. STAR Stories from Your Projects (60 min)

Prepare 4-5 STAR stories. Each should map to common behavioral questions.

**STAR = Situation, Task, Action, Result**

**Story 1: "Tell me about a challenging technical problem"**
```
Situation: In [Project], my RAG pipeline was returning irrelevant
documents 40% of the time.
Task: I needed to improve retrieval accuracy to make the app useful.
Action: I experimented with chunk sizes (1000→500 tokens),
added a reranking step using the LLM itself, and implemented
similarity thresholds to filter low-quality results.
Result: Retrieval accuracy went from 60% to 95%. Response quality
improved dramatically and the app became genuinely useful.
```

**Story 2: "Tell me about learning a new technology"**
```
Situation: I was a senior frontend engineer with no AI/ML experience.
Task: I wanted to transition to AI engineering within 4-5 months.
Action: I designed a structured program: 4 phases covering fundamentals
through production deployment. I studied 2h on weekdays, 4-6h on
weekends. I shipped 3 complete projects.
Result: In 4 months, I went from zero AI knowledge to deploying
production AI applications with RAG, agents, and tool use.
```

**Story 3: "Tell me about working under pressure / tight deadlines"**
```
Situation: Each project had a strict one-week deadline.
Task: Go from architecture to deployed, documented app in 6 days.
Action: I created a repeatable cadence: plan Monday, backend Tuesday,
frontend Wednesday, integrate Thursday, polish Saturday, ship Sunday.
Result: Shipped all 3 projects on time with live URLs, READMEs, and
demo videos. The cadence got faster with each project.
```

**Story 4: "Tell me about a time you had to make a technical decision with incomplete information"**
```
Situation: Choosing between vector databases for my RAG project —
Pinecone, pgvector, or Chroma.
Task: Pick the right one with limited experience using any of them.
Action: I evaluated each on 4 criteria: ease of setup, query performance,
cost, and production readiness. Built a minimal prototype with each.
Result: Chose [your choice] because [reason]. The project shipped
successfully and I documented my decision process in a blog post.
```

**Story 5: "Tell me about receiving feedback and improving"**
*(Use a story from your previous work experience, reframed for relevance.)*

### 3. "Why" Questions (20 min)

**"Why AI?"**
> I see AI as the most impactful technology shift since the web. After years of building traditional web applications, I wanted to be at the frontier — building products that leverage intelligence, not just display data.

**"Why are you leaving your current field?"**
> I'm not leaving it — I'm expanding it. My full-stack skills are directly applicable. I'm adding AI engineering on top of a strong foundation, not starting from zero.

**"Why this company?"**
*(Customize per company. Reference their product, mission, or technology. Be specific.)*

### 4. Practice Out Loud (10 min)

- Set a timer for 2 minutes per story
- Tell each story as if speaking to an interviewer
- Record yourself if possible — listen for filler words and rambling
- Each story should be 90-120 seconds

## Key Insight

Behavioral questions aren't about having the right answer. They're about demonstrating how you think, learn, and work. Your transition story IS the story. It shows self-direction, learning ability, and the capacity to ship under pressure. Don't be defensive about the career change — it's your biggest asset.
