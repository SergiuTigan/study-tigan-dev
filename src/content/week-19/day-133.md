## What You're Doing

Final interview prep day. More mock interviews — focused on the weak areas identified yesterday. By tonight, you should be able to walk into any AI engineer interview and perform confidently.

## The Work

### Hours 1-2: Targeted Weakness Drill (120 min)

Based on yesterday's debrief, drill your weak spots:

**If system design was weak:**
- Practice 2 more problems from scratch
- Focus on the framework: Clarify → Architecture → AI Deep Dive → Production → Tradeoffs
- Practice drawing diagrams quickly
- Time yourself: complete a design in 25 minutes

**If coding was weak:**
- Solve 5-6 more medium problems
- Focus on the pattern you struggled with
- Practice explaining your thought process while coding
- Time pressure: 15 min per problem, strict

**If behavioral was weak:**
- Record yourself answering each STAR story
- Listen back — cut the rambling, add specifics
- Practice transitions between stories
- Work on your pitch until it's effortless

**If AI questions were weak:**
- Review your Day 129 answers
- Research any topics you couldn't answer
- Practice explaining RAG, agents, evaluation with a whiteboard
- Make sure every answer references your project experience

### Hours 3-4: Full Mock Interviews (120 min)

Run 2 more complete mock interviews:

**Mock Interview #4 — "Startup Interview" (45 min)**
Simulate a fast-paced startup interview:
- 5 min: pitch + why you're interested
- 15 min: portfolio deep dive ("Walk me through your favorite project")
- 15 min: system design ("How would you build our product's AI feature?")
- 10 min: culture fit ("How do you handle ambiguity?", "Tell me about a time you shipped fast")

**Mock Interview #5 — "Technical Deep Dive" (45 min)**
Simulate a technical round:
- 20 min: system design problem you haven't practiced
- 15 min: rapid-fire AI questions (new ones)
- 10 min: coding problem

**After each mock:**
- Score yourself honestly
- Note improvements from yesterday
- Identify any remaining gaps

### Hour 5: Final Preparation (60 min)

**Create Your Interview Kit:**

Prepare a document/note you can reference before each interview:

```
MY INTERVIEW KIT

90-Second Pitch:
[Your polished pitch — bullet points, not script]

Top 3 Projects — Quick Reference:
1. [Name]: [What, Tech, Key Metric, Talk About]
2. [Name]: [What, Tech, Key Metric, Talk About]
3. [Name]: [What, Tech, Key Metric, Talk About]

STAR Stories — Mapped to Questions:
- Challenging problem → [RAG accuracy story]
- Learning new tech → [Transition story]
- Tight deadline → [One-week project cadence]
- Technical decision → [Architecture choice story]
- Receiving feedback → [Previous work story]

Questions to Ask Them:
1. AI workflow on the team?
2. Biggest technical challenge?
3. Eval framework?
4. First 90 days success?
5. [Company-specific question]

Key Numbers to Remember:
- 4 months of focused AI study
- 450+ hours invested
- 3 shipped projects
- RAG accuracy: [your number]
- Cost per interaction: [your number]
- Blog posts: [your number] published

Salary Range: $120-180K (know your floor, don't say first)
```

The remaining variable isn't more preparation — it's showing up calm, confident, and genuine. Trust your preparation. Trust your projects. Trust yourself.
