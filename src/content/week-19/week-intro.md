## Why Separate Prep Matters

You know how to build AI applications. That doesn't automatically mean you can explain it well under interview pressure. Interviews test a different skill: the ability to communicate technical decisions clearly, solve problems on the spot, and present yourself confidently.

This week, you practice that skill.

## The Interview Landscape for AI Engineers

Most AI engineer interviews include some combination of:

1. **System Design** — "Design a RAG-based support system for 10K daily queries"
2. **Coding** — LeetCode-style problems (usually easy/medium) or take-home projects
3. **AI-Specific Questions** — Evaluation, prompt engineering, architecture decisions
4. **Behavioral** — STAR stories, culture fit, "Why AI?", "Why us?"
5. **Project Deep Dive** — Walk through your portfolio, answer probing questions

## The Preparation Principle

For each topic, prepare until you can explain it confidently to a non-expert. If you can make a product manager understand your RAG architecture, you can definitely explain it to an engineering interviewer.
