## The Daily Interview Routine

### Before Each Interview (15-20 min)

**Company Research:**
- What does the company do? (Product, customers, stage)
- What's their AI angle? (Core product vs. feature vs. internal tool)
- Recent news? (Funding, launches, blog posts)
- Who are you meeting? (LinkedIn their name — know their role and background)

**Review Your Kit:**
- Refresh your pitch (customize the "why this company" part)
- Pick which project to lead with (most relevant to their product)
- Review STAR stories
- Prepare 3 company-specific questions

**Technical Setup:**
- Camera on, good lighting
- Quiet environment
- Water on desk
- Project URLs in clipboard, ready to share
- Notebook for notes during the interview

### During the Interview

**First 5 minutes — establish confidence:**
- Smile. Make eye contact with the camera.
- Deliver your pitch when asked "Tell me about yourself"
- Show genuine enthusiasm for the company (you researched them)

**Technical sections — show your thinking:**
- Think out loud. Silence is worse than wrong answers.
- Use your framework for system design
- Reference your projects naturally ("In my RAG project, I solved this by...")
- If you don't know something, say "I haven't worked with that directly, but here's how I'd approach it..."

**Behavioral sections — be specific:**
- Use STAR format
- Include numbers and results
- Keep stories to 90-120 seconds
- Connect every story back to the role

**Last 5 minutes — your questions:**
- Ask your prepared questions
- Listen carefully (these answers help you decide too)
- Thank them for their time
- Ask about next steps and timeline

### Continue Applying

Between interviews, keep the pipeline moving:
- Respond to any new outreach within 24 hours
- Submit 2-3 new applications if pipeline is thin
- Follow up on week-old applications
- Update your tracking spreadsheet

## Key Insight

Your first real interview will teach you more than a week of mock interviews. Pay attention to what questions actually get asked, what surprised you, and what felt natural. Use each interview to calibrate your preparation for the next one.
