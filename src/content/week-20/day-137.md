## What You're Doing

Last weekday of the interview sprint. Today's goal: advance every active conversation by one step. If you're in a phone screen stage, push for a technical round. If you're past technical, push for a final round. Momentum matters in hiring.

## The Work

### Advance Every Conversation

Review your pipeline and take action on each:

**Awaiting scheduling:** Send a polite nudge. "Hi [name], I wanted to follow up on next steps for the [role] position. I'm very interested and have availability [these days]. Looking forward to continuing the conversation."

**Between rounds:** If it's been 3+ business days since your last round with no word, follow up. "Hi [name], I enjoyed our conversation on [date]. Wanted to check in on the timeline for next steps."

**Have an offer (or close to one):** Use this ethically to create urgency with other companies. "I'm in late-stage conversations with a few companies and wanted to make sure we can align on timing."

**No response after 2 follow-ups:** Move on. Don't chase. Your energy is better spent on responsive companies.

### Creating Healthy Urgency

If you have multiple conversations advancing simultaneously:

- **Be honest about your timeline.** "I'm hoping to make a decision by [date]" is professional and creates natural urgency.
- **Don't bluff about offers you don't have.** It backfires.
- **Do mention that you're in active conversations.** "I'm in process with a few companies" is factual and motivating for their side.
- **Ask about their timeline.** "What does your typical timeline look like from here to offer?" helps you plan.

### Continue Applying (Yes, Still)

Even with active interviews:
- Submit 2-3 applications to maintain pipeline
- Focus on companies that excite you most
- Keep your tracking spreadsheet current

### Prep for Weekend

If offers or negotiations are coming:
- Research salary ranges (levels.fyi, Glassdoor, Blind)
- Know your floor number (the minimum you'll accept)
- Know your target number (what you want)
- Prepare your negotiation talking points for Saturday

## Key Insight

Hiring timelines vary wildly. Some startups go from first call to offer in 5 days. Some companies take 4-6 weeks. Manage your emotional energy accordingly. Stay active, stay responsive, but don't check your email every 5 minutes. The process takes however long it takes.
