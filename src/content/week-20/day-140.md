## What You're Doing

This is the last day of the program. Whether you're accepting an offer today, still in final rounds, or continuing the search — this is a moment to acknowledge what you've accomplished and set yourself up for what comes next.

## The Work

### If You Have an Offer: Accept It (60 min)

**Before accepting:**
- [ ] Offer is in writing (offer letter, not verbal)
- [ ] Base salary, equity, bonus, and benefits clearly stated
- [ ] Start date confirmed
- [ ] Negotiation complete (you asked, even if they didn't budge)
- [ ] You've said no to other active conversations (professionally)

**Accepting:**
- Sign the offer letter
- Send a warm acceptance email to the hiring manager:

```
Hi [Name],

I'm thrilled to officially accept the [Role] position at [Company].
Thank you for the opportunity and for making this process so
positive. I'm excited to contribute to [specific project/team].

Looking forward to starting on [date].

Best,
[Your name]
```

**Close the loop with other companies:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you so much for the opportunity to interview with [Company].
I've decided to accept another position, but I really enjoyed
learning about your team and product. I hope our paths cross
again in the future.

Best wishes,
[Your name]
```

### If You're Still Interviewing: That's Normal (30 min)

Job searches take time. If you don't have an offer yet:

- **Don't panic.** Average job search for engineers is 4-12 weeks of active interviewing.
- **Keep the pipeline full.** Continue applying 3-5/day.
- **Iterate on your approach.** What feedback are you getting? Adjust.
- **Your skills and projects don't expire.** They're still impressive next week and next month.
- **Consider expanding your search.** Contract roles, freelance AI projects, or consulting can bridge the gap while you find the right full-time role.

### Celebrate (120 min)

Seriously. Do something to mark this moment.

- Go to your favorite restaurant
- Buy something you've been wanting
- Tell the people who supported you what it meant
- Take a day off tomorrow
- Feel proud — because you earned it

This wasn't easy. There were days you didn't want to study. Days the code didn't work. Days the applications went into a void. You did it anyway. That matters.

## The Bigger Picture

You didn't just learn AI engineering. You proved to yourself that you can reinvent your career through discipline and execution. That skill — the ability to identify what you want, build a plan, and execute it systematically — is worth more than any single technology.

Technologies change. AI will evolve. New frameworks will emerge. But the ability to learn, build, and ship? That's permanent.

## What Comes Next

**First 90 days at the new role:**
- Listen more than you talk (first 2 weeks)
- Ship something small in week 1 (show initiative)
- Build relationships with your team
- Understand the existing codebase and AI systems
- Identify where your unique skills add the most value

**Continue growing:**
- Keep building side projects
- Stay current with AI developments (new models, techniques, tools)
- Contribute to open source
- Mentor others making similar transitions
- Write about what you learn

**Pay it forward:**
- Someone is going to start their own transition soon
- Your blog posts, your projects, your story will help them
- Be the person you wished you had when you started
