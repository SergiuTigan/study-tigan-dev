If your pipeline is thin, don't panic. Continue applying (5/day minimum) alongside interviews. Hiring cycles vary — some companies move in days, others in weeks.

## The Mindset for This Week

**You are interviewing them as much as they're interviewing you.** You're a senior engineer with a decade of experience who invested months building AI skills. You're not desperate — you're selective. That energy comes through in interviews.

**Every interview makes you better.** The first one will be rough. The third will be smooth. The fifth will feel like a conversation. Each interview is practice for the next one.

**Rejection is data, not judgment.** If you don't advance, ask for feedback. Adjust. Apply it to the next one. The process is iterative, just like everything you've built.
